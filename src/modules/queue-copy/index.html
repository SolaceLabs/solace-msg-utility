Important Considerations

  • Please do not execute this on a live queue with active publisher(s) and consumer(s).
  • The entire copy operation will stop once an error is received (e.g. insufficient quota, incorrect permissions).
  • The module takes a snapshot of the source queue prior to copying/moving to ensure the operation is bounded.
  • Destination must have sufficient spool quota.
  • Destination must have an equal or larger max message size.
  • Destination must have ingress enabled.
  • Souce queue must have egress enabled.
