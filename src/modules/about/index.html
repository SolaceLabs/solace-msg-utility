Solace Message Utility

Solace Message Utility is a browser-based tool for people who work with Solace PubSub+ event brokers: developers, testers and support teams who need to look at the messages on a queue, and the administrators who decide who may do that.

Browse a queue and read each message's properties and payload, forward or delete messages, copy messages from one queue to another, on the same broker or a different one, and search the topic subscriptions of your queues. The app is one self-contained page that runs in your browser: there is nothing to install on your machine.
