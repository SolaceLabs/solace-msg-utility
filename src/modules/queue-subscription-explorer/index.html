About

This view lists every subscription that the queues on the broker carry, across all VPNs you can see.

Use the column filters below the table header to narrow the list.

VPN and Queue accept literal substrings or * wildcards.

Subscription accepts Solace topic wildcard (* for one level, > for trailing levels) and matches by topic-set intersection. For easier searches, * in the middle of the topic is treated as a wildcard too.
